CEO MESSAGE

Nova Stage Entertainment is a newly established company founded by professionals who have built nearly two decades of experience and know-how in concert production and planning.

We have developed a strong and extensive infrastructure across the live entertainment industry, allowing us to focus on creating high-quality global productions with a solid, reliable, and professional foundation.

Nova Stage exists to deliver performances that are meticulously planned, professionally executed, and built to meet the highest standards.

Our ambition is to grow into a company that helps shape the next generation of live entertainment culture, while also developing global artists who can grow together with the stages we create.
